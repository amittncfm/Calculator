Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if I experience chest pain?

If you experience chest pain, it's important to seek medical attention immediately. Call emergency services or visit the nearest hospital. Contact us for urgent consultation with Dr. Rajesh.

How often should I have a cardiac checkup?

For preventive care, a cardiac checkup once a year is recommended for adults above 40 years. However, if you have risk factors or existing conditions, more frequent checkups may be necessary.

Is angioplasty a safe procedure?

Yes, angioplasty is a safe and minimally invasive procedure when performed by experienced cardiologists. Modern techniques and equipment have made it even safer with faster recovery times.

What are the risk factors for heart disease?

Common risk factors include high blood pressure, high cholesterol, smoking, obesity, diabetes, stress, and family history. Dr. Rajesh can assess your individual risk factors and provide preventive measures.

Can heart disease be prevented?

Many heart diseases can be prevented through healthy lifestyle choices including regular exercise, healthy diet, stress management, and avoiding smoking. Regular checkups are also crucial.

Do you offer teleconsultation?

Yes, we offer online teleconsultation for follow-up appointments and general consultations. Please contact our clinic to schedule a video consultation.
